# Licensing notes: AI-generated art (Higgsfield)

Checked: 2026-10-30
Account plan: Higgsfield **Plus** (paid)

## Summary

Commercial use of the generated images in Ballast is **allowed**, including distribution in an App Store app and in App Store screenshots.

- Higgsfield claims no ownership of inputs or outputs and does not restrict commercial use of outputs.
- Commercial-use rights are not tied to a specific plan, and no separate commercial license is needed.
- Paid plans (including Plus) download outputs without a watermark.

## Caveats

- **Training:** Under §4.4, Higgsfield may use your content (inputs and outputs) to improve its models unless you delete it. This does not affect app use, but avoid uploading private material.
- **AI training ban:** §5.2(iv) forbids using the outputs to train or fine-tune AI models.
- **No indemnification:** On Plus there is no indemnity against IP claims. Prompts here avoid real people, brands, logos and other apps' styles to reduce that risk.
- **Copyright protection:** Purely AI-generated images may get little or no copyright protection in some jurisdictions. Others could reuse them. This does not prevent using them.
- This is not legal advice. Re-check the Terms before App Store submission, because they change often.
